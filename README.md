# EXSU500_group_3
Dataset: Pawłowska, A., Ćwierz-Pieńkowska, A., Domalik, A., Jaguś, D., Kasprzak, P., Matkowski, R., Fura, Ł., Nowicki, A., & Zolek, N. (2024). A Curated Benchmark Dataset for Ultrasound Based Breast Lesion Analysis (Breast-Lesions-USG) (Version 1) [dataset]. The Cancer Imaging Archive. https://doi.org/10.7937/9WKK-Q141.
Problem: Our project aims to develop and compare machine-learning models for predicting the BI-RADS category of breast lesions from ultrasound images.
Why it matters: BI-RADS assessment guides the management of breast lesions, including decisions regarding follow-up and biopsy. An AI model capable of predicting BI-RADS categories from ultrasound images could potentially support more consistent and efficient image interpretation and clinical decision-making as well as minimizing unnecessary biopsies and associated complications.
Task type: multi-label classification
